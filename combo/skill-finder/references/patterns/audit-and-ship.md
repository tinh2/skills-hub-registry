# Pattern: audit-and-ship

Run a full audit, then fix findings and ship.

## Trigger Phrases

* audit and ship
* full audit
* quality audit before ship
* check everything and ship

## Required Steps

1. **audit.** Skill: `audit`. Purpose: end to end project audit.
2. **fix-and-ship.** Skill: `fix-and-ship`. Purpose: fix the audit findings and ship.

## Handoff Notes

* audit to fix-and-ship: pass the prioritized findings list.
